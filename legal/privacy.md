# Ripple Privacy Policy

*Draft — review before publishing. Last updated: November 2026.*

Ripple is a free social app made by Brayden (Undertow Studio). This page explains, in plain language, what Ripple stores about you, where it lives, and what you can do about it. Questions: undertowstudio48@gmail.com.

## Who can use Ripple
- Ripple is for people 13 and older. If you're younger, please don't make an account.

## What Ripple stores
- **Your account:** your username and password. Your phone scrambles the password (PBKDF2) before it is sent, and Ripple's server scrambles it again before storing it — nobody, including Brayden, can read your password. You get 8 one-time recovery codes instead of email recovery.
- **Your profile:** username, display name, bio, pronouns, profile photo, banner, links, and badges.
- **What you post:** posts, shorts, stories, comments, votes, likes, saves, follows, and the Pools (communities) you join.
- **Your messages:** chats, photos, videos, voice notes, and snaps you send.
- **Your settings:** theme, privacy, and notification choices, synced so they follow you to new devices.
- **Your interest profile:** a score per hashtag, built from what you watch, like, and skip. It helps rank For You and Shorts. You can see, edit, or reset it in Settings → Your interests.
- **Watch history:** you can pause or clear it any time.
- **Device tokens:** so Ripple can send push notifications to your phone.
- **Your birth year:** only used to check you're 13 or older. It is never shown to anyone.

## Where it's stored
- **Cloudflare:** everything runs on Cloudflare — the Worker that is Ripple's server, the D1 databases that store profiles, posts, messages, settings and your photos, videos and voice notes. No other company receives your data.
- **Notifications:** the Android app checks Ripple's server for new activity about every 15 minutes. No third-party push service is used.
- Files on public posts can be opened by anyone with the link. Private files (followers-only posts, messages, snaps, verification selfies) are only handed out after the server checks you're allowed to see them.

## Messages
- Direct messages can only be read by the people in the chat. Ripple staff don't have a "read all messages" tool.
- **Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.** They're protected in transit and by access rules on the server, but Ripple's service providers store them. Don't send anything you'd be upset to have exposed.
- If someone **reports** a message, a copy of that message goes to Ripple's moderators so they can review it.
- Snaps are deleted from storage after they're opened (or after 24 hours if nobody opens them). Screenshot alerts work on Android 14 and newer, and may not catch every screenshot.

## Verification
- To get a Verified badge you send a selfie holding a one-time code. **Ripple never asks for a government ID.**
- The selfie is private. Only Ripple's admins and owner can see it, and it's deleted 30 days after your request is decided.

## What Ripple doesn't do
- No ads. No selling or renting your data. No analytics or tracking tools.
- Ripple only shares data with the service providers above, which run the app, or when the law requires it.

## Your choices
- **Download your data:** Settings → Account → Download my data.
- **Private account:** only people you approve can follow you and see your followers-only posts.
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Account → Delete account. Your posts, the messages you sent, and your media are deleted after a 30-day grace period. Sign back in before then to cancel.
- **Notifications:** turn each type on or off in Settings → Notifications.

## The web viewer
- The Ripple website only uses cookies and local storage to keep you signed in. Nothing is used for tracking.

## Changes
- If this policy changes, the new version will be posted in the app and on the website with a new date.
